## 🧩 What I Do

### 🏛️ Platform & Product Consulting
- Translate ambiguous business goals into **clear product scope, architecture, and KPIs**.
- Define **delivery roadmaps** with milestones, costs, and risk trade-offs.
- Align stakeholders — from founders and engineers to compliance and audit.

### 💳 Fintech & Payments
- e-Wallets, payment rails, **central-bank API integrations** (CBOS Consumer API).
- KYC/AML workflows, settlement & clearing, commission engines.
- **Audit-ready architectures** built for licensing and regulatory review.

### 📱 Web & Mobile Delivery
- Modern web (Next.js, React) and mobile (React Native, Flutter) platforms.
- **REST & GraphQL APIs**, FastAPI / Node.js back-ends, CI/CD pipelines.
- UX-first product delivery — built to ship and built to maintain.

### 🛡️ Security & Reliability
- **Threat modelling (STRIDE)**, Secure SDLC, supply-chain hygiene.
- PII safety, observability, incident playbooks, rollback systems.
- Security baked into delivery — not bolted on at the end.

### 🤖 Applied AI (Where It Earns Its Place)
- **AI strategy & use-case selection** — say no to noise, yes to leverage.
- LLM integration, **RAG pipelines**, conversational AI, AI-powered development.
- Production-grade observability, evals, and cost dashboards for AI features.

## 🧠 Operating Principles

| Principle | What it means in delivery |
|-----------|---------------------------|
| 🏗️ **Build platforms, not features** | Architecture decisions today shape what you can ship in 2 years. |
| 🔐 **Security is a first-class user story** | Threat-modelled from sprint zero — not retro-fitted before launch. |
| 📱 **Mobile-first or mobile-irrelevant** | If your customers live on mobile, your platform must too. |
| 🤖 **AI only where it earns its place** | Measurable leverage or it doesn't ship. |
| 📜 **Audit-ready by default** | Especially in fintech: compliance built-in beats compliance bolted-on. |
